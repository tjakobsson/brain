Sonke Ahrens presents note-making as part of thinking and writing rather than a
separate archive. The important move is translating source material into one's
own network of claims.

That supports [[Atomic notes]] and the distinction between [[Reading notes]]
and collected highlights. It is also a direct response to
[[The collector's fallacy]].
