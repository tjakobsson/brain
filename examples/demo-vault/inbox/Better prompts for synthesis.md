Prompts should ask for pressure, not polish. Useful moves include naming the
missing premise, finding the strongest counterexample, and separating evidence
from interpretation.

Apply these prompts after [[Writing creates understanding|writing a first
explanation]]. [[Questions are durable entry points]] supplies the problem, and
[[Reading notes]] supplies the evidence.
