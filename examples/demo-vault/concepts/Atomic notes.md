An atomic note develops one idea far enough that it can be understood without
the page it came from. The useful boundary is conceptual, not a word count.

Smaller units make [[Links need context|specific links]] possible and let
[[Small notes compound]] through reuse. They also turn note-making into
[[Writing creates understanding|an act of explanation]] rather than storage.
