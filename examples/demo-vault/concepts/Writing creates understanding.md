Writing exposes missing steps that feel complete while an idea remains in
memory. A note becomes useful when the reasoning survives outside that memory.

This is why [[Atomic notes]] should be written in complete thoughts rather than
copied fragments. [[Reading notes]] supplies evidence, and
[[Better prompts for synthesis]] can help challenge a first explanation.
