A small note can participate in many arguments without being rewritten. Each
new connection increases the chance that the note will appear during later
work.

Compounding depends on [[Atomic notes|clear boundaries]] and regular
[[Evergreen note maintenance]]. A [[Weekly review]] creates a modest rhythm for
finding the next useful connection.
