The value of a note depends less on where it is stored than on whether a future
question can lead back to it. Titles, links, aliases, and repeated language all
act as retrieval cues.

[[Questions are durable entry points]] because they resemble the reader's
future search. [[Improve search vocabulary]] applies the same idea to wording,
while [[Maps of content]] provides a deliberately curated route.
