Saving information feels productive because the result is visible, but an
unprocessed collection has not yet changed what its owner can explain or do.

[[Reading notes]] should record an interpretation, not merely a highlight.
[[How to Take Smart Notes]] describes a workflow for that transition, while a
small [[Capture workflow]] keeps collection from becoming the final step.
