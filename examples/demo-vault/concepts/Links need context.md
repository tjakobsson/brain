A link without a sentence records proximity but not meaning. The surrounding
text should say whether the target supports, contradicts, applies, or extends
the current idea.

Contextual links improve [[Retrieval cues beat filing]] and make
[[Graph density and trust|graph structure]] easier to interpret. They are most
precise when they connect [[Atomic notes]].
