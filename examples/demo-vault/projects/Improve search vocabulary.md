Search misses are evidence about language. Record the phrase that failed, then
decide whether a title, alias, or sentence should include it.

The project applies [[Retrieval cues beat filing]] to the site's actual search
terms. [[Questions are durable entry points]] and [[Maps of content]] offer
alternative wording when a topic has several common names.
