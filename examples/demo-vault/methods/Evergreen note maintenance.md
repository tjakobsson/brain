Evergreen maintenance improves notes when real use exposes a problem. Rename a
vague title, add the missing premise, or replace a generic link with a sentence
that states the relationship.

This work lets [[Small notes compound]] without requiring a periodic rewrite of
the whole vault. A [[Weekly review]] can surface candidates, and
[[Links need context]] provides a practical quality check.
