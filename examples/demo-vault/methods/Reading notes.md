A reading note records what a source changed: a claim worth testing, a useful
distinction, or a disagreement. Page numbers support that interpretation but do
not replace it.

[[How to Take Smart Notes]] offers one process. The goal is to avoid
[[The collector's fallacy]] and create material for
[[Writing creates understanding]].
