Capture should be fast enough to preserve attention and incomplete enough to
invite a second pass. The inbox holds prompts, not finished knowledge.

During review, turn the useful items into [[Questions are durable entry
points|questions]] or claims, discard the rest, and connect the result. This
keeps [[The collector's fallacy]] visible. [[Weekly review]] handles anything
that does not become clear immediately.

The public example begins at [[Welcome]].
